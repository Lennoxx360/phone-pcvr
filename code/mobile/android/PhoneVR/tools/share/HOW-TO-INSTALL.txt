PHONE PCVR (alpha) - play SteamVR games on your phone in a cheap VR headset shell.
Head tracking (6DoF) uses the phone's camera via ARCore. Free and open source (GPL-3.0).

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Android 10+ phone with ARCore ("Google Play Services for AR") support
  - A Windows PC with SteamVR
  - A phone VR shell / Cardboard-style headset, with the rear camera left uncovered
  - PC and phone on the same fast Wi-Fi (5 GHz). Best: turn on Windows
    "Mobile hotspot" (5 GHz) and connect the phone straight to the PC.

1) INSTALL THE APP ON THE PHONE
   Option A (no PC tools): copy the .apk to the phone (USB, Drive, Discord...),
     tap it, and allow "Install unknown apps" for whichever app opened it.
     Play Protect may warn that the app is unknown: tap "More details" > "Install anyway".
   Option B (USB): turn on USB debugging on the phone, plug it in, and
     double-click Install-to-phone.bat.

2) INSTALL THE PC STREAMER
   Download ALVR v20.8.0 exactly (other versions won't connect):
     https://github.com/alvr-org/ALVR/releases/tag/v20.8.0
   Get the Windows streamer download, unzip it, and run "ALVR Dashboard.exe". Finish its setup wizard.
   Recommended in the dashboard settings: video codec H264, and
   "Transcoding view resolution" / emulated width around 1072 if the picture flickers.

3) CONNECT
   Open Phone PCVR on the phone and tap Start VR. In the ALVR dashboard on the PC
   the phone appears under "New devices": click Trust. Then launch SteamVR from
   the dashboard. Put the phone in the headset.

IN VR
   Gear icon: settings menu. Volume keys: tap = volume, hold Vol+ = adjust
   headset fit, hold Vol- = SteamVR menu, tap both = reset play space.

PC SETTINGS (optional but recommended)
   PC\alvr-pc-profile.ps1 sets ALVR up for this app. Close SteamVR and the dashboard first, then
   in PowerShell (use your ALVR folder, the one with "ALVR Dashboard.exe"):
     powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File PC\alvr-pc-profile.ps1 -Profile BadWifiHighEndPc -StreamerDir "C:\path\to\alvr"
   Profiles: BadWifiHighEndPc or GoodWifiLowEndPc. It backs up your settings first;
   -Profile Restore puts them back.

HAND TRACKING (experimental, off by default)
   Settings > Hand tracking. Pinch = trigger, fist = grip, left pinch-and-drag = walk.
   In the ALVR dashboard turn OFF its own hand gestures (Settings > Headset > Controllers >
   Gestures), otherwise both fire.
   Older phones: run hand tracking on the PC instead, see PC\pc-assist\README.md
   (needs Python) and turn on "Hands: PC assist" on the phone.

KNOWN ISSUES
   - Only tested on a Galaxy S24 Ultra so far. Other phones may need tweaking; please report.
   - Samsung phones drop to 60 Hz while the camera is on.
   - 6DoF pauses in the dark or facing blank walls (ARCore needs light and texture), and while a
     hand is very close to the camera.
   - Hands are only seen in front of the camera; low or far-right hands drop out.
   - Gaze pointer: the SteamVR dashboard laser doesn't always show up.

This is an early alpha. Expect rough edges.
